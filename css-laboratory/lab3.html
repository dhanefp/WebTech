Styling Text, Colors, and Backgrounds

Why Readability Matters

Readable text keeps visitors on a page. Good font sizes, enough line spacing, and strong contrast between text and background make content easier to scan and understand.

A consistent color scheme also helps users recognize headings, links, and important information at a glance.

Tips for Better Styling

  • Use a clean sans-serif font for body text.
  • Keep paragraph text around 16px.
  • Limit your palette to a few colors.
  • Check that text contrasts with its background.
